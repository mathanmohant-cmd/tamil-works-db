💡 Quick Tips

  • Case matters! Small (t, n, l) vs Capital (T, N, L, R)
  • Unique Tamil: zh = ழ, R = ற, n2 = ன
  • Long vowels: aa or A → ஆ
  • Three N sounds: n (ந), N (ண), n2 (ன)
  • Examples: thirukkural → திருக்குறள், azhagu → அழகு
